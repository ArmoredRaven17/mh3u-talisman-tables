## Game data and icons

The talisman generator and its tables were worked out by the author from a copy of the game
extracted from a personally owned cartridge. `docs/data.js` holds the tables the generator reads
and the skill, charm and talisman names it shows. The monster icons in
[docs/assets/MonsterIcons/](docs/assets/MonsterIcons/) are the game's own, shared with the
author's other MH3U apps. **No game files, archives or keys are redistributed.**
