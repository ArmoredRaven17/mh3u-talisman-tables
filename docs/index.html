How to use it

In MH3U every save is stuck on one of 17 talisman tables from the moment you press New Game. Your table decides which talismans your charms can ever turn into. Twelve tables are normal. Five are cursed (T11, T12, T15, T16, T17) and can only give a few hundred different talismans.

Browse a Table: pick a table and see every talisman it can give, and which charms give it. Narrow it down by charm, skill, points and slots. Best rolls only keeps just the top versions.

Find a Talisman: enter a talisman to see which tables can give it. Enter several of your own talismans and only your table, or a few, will still fit. Tick or better to check whether a table can give a talisman at least that good.

Mystery Charms never have a second skill, and only Timeworn and Enduring Charms can have 3 slots.

MH3U Talisman Tables

An unofficial, non-commercial fan tool for Monster Hunter 3 Ultimate. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by Capcom.

Where the lists come from. The skill lists, point ranges, slot odds and talisman names were read from the game's own code. The app then rolls every charm from every spot on every table, exactly as the game does.

Checked against a real save. All 258 talismans in a long-played save are on that save's table, down to the slots and the talisman name.

Table numbers. These are the community's numbers, matched with the 13 published New Game times. Nobody published times for T12, T15, T16 and T17, so those four are numbered by convention, the same way as in the MH3U Cursed Table app.

Background from the GameFAQs, Neoseeker and MH Wiki charm-table threads.
